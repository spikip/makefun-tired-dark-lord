## About this entry

The brief asks for a villain who is secretly just very tired, and for the
tiredness to live in the voice rather than in a plain announcement of it,
so this monologue never once says the word tired. Instead the Dark Lord
keeps office hours, arrives with a to-do list instead of thunder, and has
pawned the amulet of endless night because the goblin landlord does not
accept prophecies and the rent on the mountain was due. His grand scheme
is still intact - the eclipse, the coronation, the whole dark ballet - but
it is scheduled for Tuesday, and it will rain, because it always rains on
Tuesdays now. The monologue closes on the smallest possible act of
defiance: conquering the realm can wait until he has sat down with a cup
of tea, and the realm, he has checked twice, is not going anywhere. It is
exactly one hundred words, so every sentence has to earn its place, and
the program checks both the word count and the six hundred character cap
before writing the text to /output/entry.txt.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the one-hundred-word monologue to `/output/entry.txt`.
